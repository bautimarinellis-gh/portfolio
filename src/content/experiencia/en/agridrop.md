## Problem

A new agri-inputs brand with no digital presence. They sell to agronomists and farmers, not end
consumers, so they needed a site that looked technically serious: certifications and real
specifications for each product.

## Content decision

Instead of generic marketing copy, each product shows its technical sheet (active ingredient,
compatibility, pH and percentage of active component). The sheets were provided by the company;
my part was organizing and presenting them on the site.

## Scope

A production landing page with hero, a catalog of the 5 product lines (Drop Forte, Drop Thor, Drop
Flow, Drop Elixir, Drop Quality) with technical sheets and ISO 9001/14001 certification, an "about"
section and direct contact through WhatsApp. Plus social media management (Instagram/Facebook), email
marketing, and a database of agronomy businesses filtered across 5 provinces for commercial
outreach, built separately from the site.
